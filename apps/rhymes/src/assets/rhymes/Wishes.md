11.10, And I look at the clock
As the hands of time begin to chime.
I await for the moment,
To ask for something precious.
A myth they say,
But it gives me another chance of hope,
Hope, thats stands through time.


11.11, and now it's time to make a wish!
A wish to make my worries disappear,
A wish to manifest thoughts   into something that exists.
With all my might,
I wish for something to be true,
By magic I suppose,
But by my efforts, on my own, the spells I'll brew.

11.12, and now the wish has been made.
Now I shall work hard through hammers and blades,
And look for the stars as they align.
I shall shine with them through age and time.
"What did you wish for?"
Someone's curiosity might say,
But I shall not disclose my wishes as the stars might fade away.
