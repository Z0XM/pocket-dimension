mai joke suna dunga,
mai poem suna dunga.

taare to nahi tod paunga,
magar apne taaron pe sair kara dunga.

unhi taaron ke mei jab tootega kuch,
apni khwaishe hatake tumhari khwaishe saja dunga.

mai joke suna dunga,
poem suna dunga,
mai agli baar "hmm" ki jagah "okay sanju very good" chilla dunga.

bas in sab ke baad,
thoda sa muskura dena,
agli baar ussey halki si aur muskaan ke liye,
phirse yahi likh ke dikha dunga
