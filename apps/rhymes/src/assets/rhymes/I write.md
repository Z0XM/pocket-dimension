After seeing that special someone walk across,
"I fall".
Along with a million unnoticed eyes, towards them,
"I look".
And then suddenly! When they glance at me,
"I hide".

To not be suspicious, obviously, In a random direction,
"I walk"
Me, being a fan of chess, In a straight line,
"I rook".
Walking home, in sweet and pleasant  dreams with them,
"I ride".

To tell all of it, to let them know the meaning of love, a friend,
"I call".
Day dreaming about them, all others things, obviously,
"I overlook".
And ofcourse I do not have the courage to meet them and express so,
"I write".
