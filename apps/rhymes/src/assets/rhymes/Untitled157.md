Wo kya hai na,
Din mere thode acche jaa na re the,
Sapno mei aap thoda sata rahe the,
Isliye,
Exams mei bhi marks mere aa na re the,

Paas waas hone ke liye kuch karna hoga,
Saamne wo dekha professor jaa rahe the,
Black vlack mail ab kuch karna hoga,
Class B ki shreya ke saath samose kha rahe the
