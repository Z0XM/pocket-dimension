So, I spent the entire day working from 11 am till 8 pm without talking with anyone or resting. Then papa ne movie ki tickets book kardi to 9 baje jana pada. We were 7 people in the car. Everyone was talking with each other having normal conversations like normal people. And then there was me just looking outside the window. Some 1 year ago, when  I was in a situation like this I used to secretly text a certain someone and eagerly wait to get out of this and get the chance to talk with that person. Alas! Now I have no one I would wanna wait for.
It's not like I miss that person. I miss the feeling of waiting for someone knowing that they must be waiting for you too. I wish I had that. I wish I had someone to talk to. I wish I had someone who would pick up my calls. I wish I had someone who wanted to talk to me. I wish a lot of things. Maybe one day these whishes will come true. Till then, all I can do is wait and keep looking out of the window.
