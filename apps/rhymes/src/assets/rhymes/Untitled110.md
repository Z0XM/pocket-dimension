Agar chaand ki thodi si roshani chura sakun,
To mai wapas lauta dunga.

Agar paas tumhare thoda aa sakun,
To mai daayre badha dunga.

Agar nazaron se tumhara Rukh bata sakun,
To nazrein hata lunga
