A joke has been made,
And as you laugh with so much joy,
There it comes,
Your pretty and big of a smile.

You see someone struggle,
Help them with all your might,
And even if it hurts you bad,
For them, it shows up again,
Your sweet and natural of a smile.

You struggle a lot within yourself,
But to not let others get a glimpse of it,
You keep it happy,
And hence it comes,
Your lit and lovely of a smile.

And to forget all of it,
You act so vibrant,
So jolly and energetic,
To top it all,
There it comes, My favorite,
Your stupid and crazy of a smile.

And now when its the worst of it all,
Still you stand strong,
Not letting Yourself fall.
And even with water in your eyes,
Its still pretty, sweet, lit and stupid as it can be,
Your big, natural, lovely and crazy of a smile
