I wish I was next to you,
Not to make you feel better or make you smile,
But rather so that I can see how you are,
So that I can see you through your eyes.

I wish I was next to you,
Not that it would make your problems dissappear,
But maybe I can help create some memories in the difficult times,
Memories, which you would hold dear.

I wish I was next to you,
I don't know if even it would matter,
But for the chance of it, I would wish it,
If by any way I can make you any happier.

Now, it is not the only wish I have,
I want to have love, life and riches too.
But for what its worth,
When you wake tomorrow morning and read this,
I wish! I wish I was next to you.
