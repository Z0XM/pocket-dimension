aane se, jaane se,
kya hi hai hona.

kya mai karun ab,
kyu bematlab ka rona,

tum ho yahan pe par,
baaton ka na hona,

mujhko kyu lagta hai ab ye sahi hai,
kyunki tum jo chaho ab wo yahi hai.
