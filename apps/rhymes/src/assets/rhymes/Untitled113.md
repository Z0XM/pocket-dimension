Mai jo chal raha tha raaste mein,
chalte chalte gir gaya mai,
girte girte uth gaya mai,
uthte uthte ruk gaya mai,
rukt rukte badh gaya mai,
badhte badhte socha maine,
ye kya ho raha hai mujhe?
kyu ud raha hu mai ?
kyun ye vichar mere,
mujhko hi lachar karte ?
kya ho raha hai mujhe ?
kyun likh raha hu mai ?
aate jaate log puche,
kyu has raha hu mai?
