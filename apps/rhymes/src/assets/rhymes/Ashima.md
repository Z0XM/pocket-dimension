If this was a dream let's watch it together,
If this was a painting let us paint forever.

If this was a wish let us not lose hope,
If this was a gift let us keep it closed.

For if we stop watching,
Stop painting,
Lose hope,
Or decide to see what it is,

We will come out of our imaginations,
Our lives with dreamy translations.

Let us draw out what feels unreal,
Let us paint how we see us.

And for the gift,
Let it be unknown, a curious mystery.
As when we meet again, 
Let it be a reason for the fun
And for another page in our history.
