Don't turn your face away,
Don't hide behind those smiles,
I know you are the prettiest in the town,
But along with your "beauty bones",
I hope you let me see you frown.

You smile so often,
Shine more than the sunrise,
But even if the dusk settles,
I'll still look at you the same.
As you will still shine.
Maybe not as sunshine,
Maybe as a moonlight?
But even if you don't shine,
Even if the night's dark,
I'll hold onto your stars,
And won't let them slip afar.

And for the worst,
If still, there are tears in your eyes,
I'll let them be.
As I know by how strong you are,
There'll be multiple sunrise,
Even before a sight of the dark.
