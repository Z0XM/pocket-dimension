Everything will seem fine,

Once the days are gone and seen as not what we'll lose
But rather as what we had.

Let the winds of time blow,
With them you must chime.

In the moments that are yet to come,
Let yourself shine.

Everything will seem fine,
When these days turn into memories,
And these memories get replaced by new memories.
And all that is left of these days are the rhymes.

Soon,
Everything will seem fine.
