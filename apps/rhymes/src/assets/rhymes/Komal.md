Komal si ye Nazar,
In nazaron mei komal se khwaab.

Komal se ye kadam,
In kadamon mei milon hazaar.

In khwaabon aur milon mein rang hazaron hain,
Is bhuri zameen mein,
Neele aasmaano mein,
Lal se phoolo mein,
Kaali si raaton mein,
Narangi subahon mein
Kitne tumhare hain?

In rangon se saare tasveere jo banti hai,
Kuch khush,
Kuch niraash,
Kuch bindaas,
Kuch pareshan.
In tasveeron mei sabse komal jo lagti hai,
Saare rangon sabse  sundar wo, shweta.
