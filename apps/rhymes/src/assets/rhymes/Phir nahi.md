wahi jo pehle kabhi hua tha,
wahi jo ab to nahi,
tumse milke kuch aisa laga tha,
phir kabhi nahi,
bas pehli dafa hi dil mera hasa tha,
ab to kuch hota nahi,
abhi batado jo kuch bhi hai kehna,
milunga phir nahi
