agar kal bhi hote tum,
to mai tumko sab batati ,
mera dil bhi khush hojata,
aur aankhen nam na phir ho pati.

agar kal bhi hote ab hum, 
to loriyan tumhe sunati.
bin zarurat ki raaton meintere dil ko rahat dilati.

sath rahenge hum jab tak,
sang chalenge falak tak, 
chalte chalte din,
dhale dhalte,
unkaheen baatein ka karenge kab tak.


agar kal bhi hoti tum,
saari baatein sunne aata,
tere dil mei chupke aake,
tere gham sab leke jaata
