In sun-shining days
With you faraway
I still see you around in crowded streets.
With a smile so pretty
And anger so gritty
How are you so flowers and sweets?

A look at your face,
At the start of my days,
Makes me go sleep, and see you again,
Again and again, back in my dreams.

Every word that you say, feels like a poem,
Every song that you sing,
Feels like I am listening,
Alive in an heaven, to angels with wings.

Your cheeky little jokes,
All lame and so forced,
Makes me smile anyways, and then you find peace.
Why is all of this,
Only true in a dream?
Why does the life I live,
Is not sweet as in the song it all seems.
