I lie on my bed, turn off the lights.
Tuck in the bedsheet and
Crawl on to the side
As I go deep into the sleepless nights.

As I dream into the sleepless nights?

I wonder if I'll even have a bed by my side,
For a comfortable eternal sleep,
As I go deep into the sleepless nights.
