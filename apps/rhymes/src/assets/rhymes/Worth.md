“Use words to prove your worth first”

Oh dear stranger,
You ask me my worth.
The same I wonder,
As everyone calculates for me since birth.

Oh dear stranger,
You could have asked what I like.
I would have answered in lots,
Of the interests that arise.

Oh dear stranger,
You could have asked what I wish for,
I would have not shyed away
But shown you the dreams in my eyes.

Oh dear stranger,
Now how do I know,
What worth am I?
I guess my worth could be known through the people I connect,
But then again, how would I know before I die ?

Oh dear stranger,
How do I decide?
Whats not and whats of Worth?
I am just living this life happily,
Passing my days on this boring earth.

Oh dear stranger,
As I write these rhymes,
I guess I know!
My worth is for the connections I have made
And the love in their hearts,
As I stand by them in heat and in shade.

Oh dear stranger,
You ask me my worth,
And through words I shall prove.

The same I wonder,
And now these thoughts will take up all the space in my mind's room.

Oh dear stranger,
You ask me my worth,
Would I be any happier?
If my worth was something I knew?
