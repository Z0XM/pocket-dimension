If I knew what's wrong
Maybe, I would live a better life.
In my head, If I knew,
How to love and tell it out loud
It would be louder than the noise.

The seeds of love,
To be sown with love,
Are something unseen, unfelt,
With hopes of growth everyday.
As they can only be planted by others,
As I fail to live the love within myself.

The seeds of love,
To be grown with love,
Deep within, yet unfound,
Its waiting for the waters of "I love you".

I love the way you look,
I love the way you walk.
I love it how with a small effort,
I always make you smile.
And I await for you to do the same, to tell me the same,
For you to fit in for me,
Make small efforts, Tell me a joke sometimes.
Even If I feel no different, I would still smile for you tried.

I love to sit silently with you,
Would you love it too ?
Or Is it boredom for you, as it shows.
And alone, one-sided, my love grows.

I dreamt,
I dreamt to find a bond,
To feel the love and why is it an emotion so strong.
I thought I could feel it in my mind,
Water the seeds myself,
Maybe with the sunlight of hope it grows along.
But If I gave all in for self,
How would I make you smile?
How would I help you feel loved?
How would I make you feel okay, When you feel its all wrong?

The seeds stay, as they were I think
Waiting,
Deep alone they exist,
To be watered by you,
And as I am reaching my end,
I doubt, If they were ever there to begin with!
