I was looking up at the night sky,
I found something was missing....

Then, I looked at you,
Sitting right beside me.
A place, reserved only for you,
Where, I hope, you'll always be.

I wondered, how could you shine so bright?
How could you smile even in the darkest Night?
If I was a poet, I would compare you to a star,
That came down from afar.

I saw your hand resting on the grass,
And though its been some time, since we're together,
Still I am somewhat shy ,
To hold your hand and let the moment be forever.

Slowly I crawled my fingers towards yours,
Grabbed your hand and wrapped mine around.
The experience was as nerve-wrecking,
As romantic it sounds.

Then Suddenly you turned your face,
Looked at me.
Panicked, I looked away!

The Moment was bound,
Then Again, I looked at the sky,
Something was missing,
Oh right! One star came down.
