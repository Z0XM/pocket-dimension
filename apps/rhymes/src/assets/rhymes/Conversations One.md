Z:
If what I say could speak 1000 ways,
Which of them would you choose to walk?

R:
What if I go 1 by 1?
Is my time so brief to walk them all?

If you pebble your 100 stories,
And unable to decipher, feeling confused, I turn away?

Would your stones still keep rolling as the night rolls over the day?

Z:
I have a lot of fables, for you, I'll read them 1 by 1,
You might like some,
You might sleep on others,
But with each new day, and each new night, the pages would still turn.

So,
Would you walk along the same path I walk?
To hear the stories that are told across,
Without the worry of the path being right or wrong?

R:
With the infinite steps I take, can I bring your stories along ?
I wanna be strong for the soul that is yours.
And I apologize, For if I annoy you with my talks.

Even if I do,
I wanna be strong enough to make you smile.
But What if,
Second chances are not for me?
And What if,
I have one shot and the chance is lost in time?
And what if,
I was replacable all this while?
Maybe!
A possibility to see,
But replacable? For me you'll never be

Z:
With Zero fears put your thoughts forward,
Talk, annoy or whatever you may wanna name it,
I would still have fun with your countless random words.

And when you may,
I would smile in the least of efforts,
Count away,
But I do not, hence your chances seem endless,
And even as countable,
As a chance, I do not see,
Its you, living all factorials of life as in sweet fables,
And for you being replacable,
A thought to cross?
A possibility?
Rather the circumference of a coin that it lands on in a toss!
