Feels like no one cares for me,
Feels like no one's here for me.

If I show this to you you may rush to me,
See if everything's okay, show your love to me,
Ask if something bad happened, console me,
Tell me that 'this' is not your version of me.

I have another side of me,
Being awesome with everyone I see.

My friends are awesome,
We spend a lot of good time together.
Do I enjoy? If you may ask,
Yes he does, not me.

I have been developing a lot of things,
Winning a lot of contests,
Getting recognized as the smartest kid.
Do I celebrate? If you may ask,
Yes he does, not me.

I think I have become somewhat of a man.
Not because I spend my time working,
Not because I care for people around me,
Not because I am trying to earn,
Not because I have learnt to lie,
But maybe as I forgot to cry.

Even If I did cry,
Who would know?
Who would see beside the face I show?

But, why do I need someone to see?
'Happiness being alone is what you should seek'
Isn't that what people say?

I have friends,
I have laptop,
I have internet,
I have money,
I am somewhat smart,
I score good,
I think I make others smile,
Still..
Am I happy? If you may ask,
Yes he is, not me.

I don't know what I lack,
I don't know what I need,
With time I will heal,
Console myself, smile again,
At least, that's how it has been.
