for the day,

haan mujhe kuch samajh na aaye,
meri soch kyun mujhse hi paheliyan bhujaye,

would it make it easier,
if i - i go high and just die,

if i say a final goodbye.

"bro your life's so perfect , I wish i was just like you"

maine,
maine na socha tumse hogi mulakaat
