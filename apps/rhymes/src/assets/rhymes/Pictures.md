If you ask me to capture the moment to make feel a bit alive,
And to hold it dear for many upcoming years.

I would rather capture the look in your eyes and the warmth in your touch.

For when I recollect the moment,
I shall not remember this moment through digital lies.
I shall remember not only the clouds but also the sunrise through the haze of your eyes
