Kabhi narm, kabhi tez,
kabhi garm, kabhi sard,
tu leher leher v-aayu-si

Teri baatein mushkil se samajh aaye,
Tere shabd paheliya ban jaye.
Teri ankaheen baatein sunne ke liye phir bhi taiyaar hai,
Bas tu ek baar Bat-aa-yushi.

Sabse alag, sabse juda tu,
In panktiyo mei bayan na kare paun jo hai tu,
Kabhi fursat mei bata dun,
Uske liye just wait for the time till Aay(I)-shi(see)-yu
