Tu thoda pyaar mat karna,
Mai na karpaunga.
Tu mera haath na pakadna,
Main na chal paunga.
Tu mere sath na theherna,
Main na ruk paunga.

Tu meri chahton ka dariya,
Mere ashkon ka tu zariya,
Meri aakhon mei na takna,
Mai simat jaungaa.

Tu thoda pyaar mat karna,
Mai na kar paunga.
