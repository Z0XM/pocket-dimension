All I can do is lie before you,
All I can do is smile before you.
To not let us be any closer, I may act rough,
To present my true self, I am not strong enough.
Not strong enough to love you as I would,
Not strong enough to care for you as I would.
And thus, a sweet friendship is what I would never have,
Though, I wish I could.
