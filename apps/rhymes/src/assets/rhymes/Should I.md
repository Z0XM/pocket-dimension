I say "I love", but should I ?
For if the world doesn't love me, why should I ?
I say "I listen", but do I ?
Do I just listen to the voice approaching?
Or your voice which seems lost in a noise.
I say "I care" , Really ?
Then why from a warm hug, I always shy ?
I always say "I am the best!",
But with all these thoughts I wonder, Am I ?

And now with no one to love,
No one to listen to,
And no one for whom I care,
For no one to read this,
"I write"!
But should I ?
