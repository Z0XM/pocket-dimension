I hope you are fine,

I hope you are fine in the world I can see,
I hope you are fine in the world I can be.

I hope you are fine with tears in your eyes,
Even in your cries,
I hope you are fine behind your cheeky smiles,

I hope you are fine in your sickness,
In your weakness,
In the cold and fever,
I hope you are fine through all when you rise up to your endeavours.
