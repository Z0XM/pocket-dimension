Ye dooriyan hai jo kam hoti jaa rahi hai.
Magar ye faasla kyu badhta mujhko dikh rha hai.
Teri nazar Idhar udhar kyu jaa rahi hai.
Jab mere saamne ek tu bas dikh raha hai.
Mere aaine mein tu parchai meri
Tere is aaine mein kya tujhko dikh raha hai
Ye tera waqt jo hai tez tez badh raha hai
Kyun mera waqt tham raha mujhko lag raha hai

Tu meri zindagi mein, mai teri zindagi mein,
Is ghar mein hum saath saath hi aaye the na,
Alag alag yun kamro mei hum baithe hai ab,
Ye kamre alag alag hi rahenge ab kya.
Aur mera dil kuch tujhe batana chahe,
Par baat sunne ke liye aas paas tu na.
Tu apni zindagi mein khoya hua lag raha hai
Is ghar se jaa raha mai, kya tujhko dikh raha hai.
