You do great things, amazed by the actions, I join my hands, praise you for my life's span.
I might walk right beside you, help in any way I can, learn in all ways I can.

You show me great things,
Done and dusted in the past,
Stories of history,
In the realm of magic and religion they last.

Now, should I do great things,
Or subdue myself only in the greatness of great beings?

Beings, with
Two eyes, One mind,
Same as mine,
But I am told they are great as they worship the divine
