Wo kya hai na,
Hum sirf dost hai.
Mai tujhe zyada kuch bata nahi sakta,
Mai tujhse zyada abhi jata nahi sakta,

Tum kehte to ho ki pyaar hai,
Batate bhi ho,
Jatate bhi ho,
Magar,
Kuch baat karne tujhe paas mai bula nahi sakta,
Paas baithakar dil ko sehla nahi sakta,
Wo kya hai na,
Hum sirf dost hai

Teri aakhon se ashqon ko mita nahi sakta,
Tere lafzo ki maayusi jhata nahi sakta,
Tere sapno ko sach se saja nahi sakta,
Teri khwaishon ko apne sapne banane ka haq jata nahi sakta.
Kyun?
Wo kya hai na hum bas dost hai.

Teri nazron ke hazaro nazaro mein,
Mai drishya apne basa nahi sakta.
Ye jo shaamein tere saath ki hai,
Un shaamon mei sadiyan bita nahi sakta.
Ye waqt kyun mai Thama nahi sakta?
Tu jaaa raha hai zara zara,
Mai yu darr raha hu har pal har sama,
Hamari dosti ke bikharne ke darr se kyun tujhko dara nahi sakta.
Theek hai sadiyan to nahi par kya kuch aur shaamein tere sath bhi ab mai bita nahi sakta?

Kya hum abhi bhi...?

In khayalon mein,
khwaabon mein
Meri ankhon ke nazaron mein,
Hai kayi in behti baharon mein
Magar milega kahan tumhare jaisa
In rishton ke bazaron mein.

Ab to kuch bataya jaata hi nahi,
Ab to kuch jataya jaata hi nahi,
Ab sajane ke liye koi sapna bhi nahi,
Aur kehlane ke liye tu apna bhi nahi.
Kehne ke liye hum jaankar to hai,
Magar bitane ke liye sadiyan, shaamein, ya kuch pal bhi nahi.
Wo kya hai na,
Ab hum dost bhi nahi.

Magar fir bhi,
Jab arso mei koi ek mulaqat ho,
Nazron mei nazare tumhare abaad ho,
Chahe bin ashqon ke kisi baat ka dhukh mehsoos ho,
Us ek pal bhi,
Tumhari muskaan se zaroori kuch nahi,
Tumhari aankhon ki chamak se tez roshani kuch nahi.
Apne khayal, sawal aur pareshaniyan hatake,
Itni sadiyon ke baad bhi,
Agar mehsoos kara paya ki kuch badla nahi,
Shayad kehla paunga apne aap ko ek achcha dost tabhi.
Wo kya hai na,
Hum dost the kabhi
