zaroori lag rha hai,
Tera ab sath.
Najane kaise,
Karun mai ikraar.
Hone laga hai,
Thoda sa pyaar

Haan thoda sa hua hai mujhe pyaar,
Zyada na hojave is baar,
Ke zyada na hojave,
Ke dil tut jaaye,
Ke nazar bhi na aaye phir mera yaar.
Zyada na hojaye mujhe pyar, phir ek baar.

Kyun Humko dheree dheere sath mei ghulna tha ?
Kyun chalti bheedh mei is bheedh se bichadna tha ?
Kyun teri raaho mei phir raah chalte takna tha ?
Kyun chalti raaho mei saath saath chalna tha ?

Kya ye khayal sirf mujhko aate hai ?
Inke jawab kya sirf mujhe hi satate hai ?
kya tu bhi chahta hai roz roz milna yun ?
Ya ittefaq se hi hum mil jaate hai ?

Karne laga hu,
tera intezaar.
aankhon mei teri,
dikhe thoda pyaar.

Thode se jo geet tere dil ko chuhe hai,
Thode se ishare tere preet hue hain,
Thodi si hasi jo teri mujhko dikhti hai,
Meri Dhadkanen tere geet likhti hai.

Ab kyu lag rahi,
thodi kami?
Jab tu hai yahaan,
Aur mai bhi yahin.
rehne laga,
khoya khoya mai,
pura pura mai,
Maine Pehle kyun socha nahi.

Ki thoda pyar zyada mujhe hogaya to?
Mai aankhon mei teri kho gaya to?
Mai baahon mei teri so gaya to?
Mai sath tere ro gaya to?
Par phirse agar wo na hua jo socha tha sapno mei har ek dafa?

Ye sapne kyun mujhko satate hain ?
Ye sapne kyun meri khwaishe sajate hain ?
khwaishe tujh tak jo pohoche to sun lena.
ek tarfa pyaar ka ye rishta tum chun lena.
