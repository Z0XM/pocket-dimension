If your grandkids ask you,
"Who wrote this poem ?"
You would tell them its me,
By the day with me that you would remember.

A day to remember
Would be the day we met,
In a new place and among all new faces,
Behind masks of safety,
And a share of generosity,
Our friendship was set.

A day to remember
Would be when we used to have lunch,
So much to talk about and so many flavors,
And lots of random fun.

In all of those days,
I still was somewhat shy.
And you had a space of yourself
Not allowed to many,
But with soft understandings,
In that space of yours, sometimes, I could stand by.

A day to remember
Would be when in the blast of celebration,
We exchanged personal situations.
It left a mark, on my view of you.
Crazy and bubbly as you may be,
You still are Aishwarya, with a soft and strong heart,
Which makes you "you".

A day to remember
Would be the event of halloween.
A crazy day, a first for me.
And an ending as perfect as warm as it could have ever been.

A day to remember
Would be filming you so many times,
An experience as a director,
All for a reel and crazyy vibes.

A day to remember
Would be dancing with you.
The only dance I properly know,
And among all of the others,
The only one who remembers the steps till now is you.

A day to remember
Would be the pretty and cozy ethnic day.
From shooting and trying ramp walks with you,
To actually doing a ramp walk,
That day made me come a long way.

Through all of those days,
Your trust and care for me was constant, only more to grow,
As I developed and understood the world around,
As I learned to  express, feel emotions, and tried a little to show.

Some days to remember
Would be me with my long hair,
And you with your colors,
Me with your iPhone,
And at the end of that day,
You with only a thousand pictures

A day to remember
Would be us painting tshirts.
A day to remember
Would be us graduating from college.
A day to remember
Would be us on a date, that didn't happen.

A day to remember
Would not be today
As this day is filled with goodbyes.
A day to remember
Would not be today
And if this letter makes you remember it, throw it away.

But then how would you remember me?
Honestly,
I don't even know if these are the days you'll remember me by.
And for when your grandkids ask of me,
Maybe you can introduce me as,
"The uncle who used to make you smile”
