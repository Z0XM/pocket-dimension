If I was a flower, Would I withstand winds flowing strong?
Or break apart in the chaos of rain and thunderstorms?
If I was a flower, Would I bring you joy?
Or cut through your bright smiles with my natural thorns?

When you look at me with the hazy glare,
I question, If I should be the flower so rare.
With the shining sun and a soft breeze I tend to sway,
But so do all other flowers to compare.

The more you look at me,
The more I shy away.
Just a plant on the field,
The beauty must be your eyes to say.
For you see me more colorful than I am,
And moreover when my true colors are not at play.
In your eyes, joyous, hopeful and admirable, I might be today,
But I feel I am not a flower yet,
Just a bud on the way
