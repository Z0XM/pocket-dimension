Dances of us can't last quite long,
Unknown to me, they end before the good part of our song.

Songs of us, can't rhyme all along,
But at least we sing, with the lyrics all gone wrong.

Voices of us are unheard, in the chaos they are lost.
I wonder if we listen to each other atleast in our thoughts?

Thoughts of us, for each other, do not cross the same bounds,
Bounds, set by memories , complex for me, that go all around.

Memories of us bundle moments of love.
Love that exists with no expectations of us.

Love of us, non-existential as it may be,
Sometimes, allows us to just be and dance across life.

And,
Dances of us can't last quite long,
Unknown to me, they end before the good part of our song.
