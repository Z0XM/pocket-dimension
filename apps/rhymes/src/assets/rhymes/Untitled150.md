Jeb hai khali meri, nikla hu ishq kamane
In apno ke beech bhi, na jane kyun mujhe na koi pehchane
Raat ko yaadon mei teri, der se soya tha
Subah der se utha yaadon mei teri
Aur peeche se aawaaz aayi,
" Aaye babuchak, nahane jaa nahane”
