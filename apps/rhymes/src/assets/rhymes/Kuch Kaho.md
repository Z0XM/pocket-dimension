kuch kaho, kuch bhi kaho,
ab na tum saath mei chup yun raho,
is pal mujhe kyun tarsa rahe,
khaamoshi ki baarish sunne to do.

kya kahu,
mujhko pata na kyun khaamosh hu.
baatein jo dil mei wo aati nahi,
man ko mere satati wahi,
phir khaamoshi hi ab bachi hai,
sukoon bhi yahi, saza bhi yahinn.
