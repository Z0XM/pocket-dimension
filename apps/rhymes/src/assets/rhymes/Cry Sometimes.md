I look from afar,
See them cry,
To express a little
And make themselves feel a bit fine,
They cry.

They say it brings emotions out
And helps one to feel better.
And I agree, I advise others the same,
But then for myself why do I doubt?

What harm would it do to say I love?
What harm would it do to say I miss?
What harm would it do to hug and burst out?
What harm would it do to express  sometimes?
Right now, all I can express is through my rhymes.
And hence, I ask myself several times,
With no answers to be concluded as it all conflicts in my mind.

With this perfect life I have crafted,
There are days when I am unhappy,
There are days when I feel lost,
There are days in which the night keeps me awake,
There are days in which the most troublesome are my thoughts.

And hence, to seem a little better in their eyes,
I wonder,
Should I cry sometimes?
