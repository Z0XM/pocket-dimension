Do pal ki khushi hai ye ya do pal ka gham,
Pyar to tha na hamare beech?
Phir bas sath kyun do pal the hum?
Abhi lag rha hai jee lun phir se wo do pal,
Gham to hoga is waqt ke baad,
Lekin khusi bhi to hai inhi do pal.
