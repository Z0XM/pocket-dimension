In andheri raaton mei na ye sitaare gina tu akshata,
In behti nadiyon ke na ye kinaare dikha tu akshata.

Apni baaton mei na ye ishare chupa tu akshata,
Apni baaton se na ye khwab saja tu akshata.

Meri baatein to ek dayere tak seemat reh jayengu,
Meri baatein to tujhe kabhi lubha na payengi,

Meri baaton se na ho yun khata tu akshata,
Apni pyari pyari baatein de bata tu akshata,

In baaton se baandh de sama tu akshata,
Gas pe rakhe cooker ko aanch de zara tu akshata,

Chawal adhoore reh gaye to kachche lagenge akshata,
Aur kachche dhaago mei bhi hum poore rahenge sada akshata.
