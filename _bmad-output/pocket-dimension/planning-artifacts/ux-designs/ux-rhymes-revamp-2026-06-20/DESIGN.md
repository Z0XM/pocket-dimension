# Brand & Style

`rhymes` should feel like a contemporary literary room: dark, quiet, tactile, and expressive without looking decorative for its own sake. The shell should be restrained so the writing can carry personality. Public chrome should lean off-black and off-white. Content itself may become more expressive through creator-applied formatting.

## Colors

- Base background: warm near-black
- Surface background: charcoal and ash variants
- Primary text: soft off-white
- Secondary text: dim stone gray
- Border/divider: muted graphite
- Accent usage: minimal and mostly neutral by default

Design rule: the system shell stays calm; content may become vivid in controlled ways inside the reading surface.

## Typography

- Default body typography should prioritize reading comfort, not novelty.
- Titles should support expressive typography but within a curated font set in the first phase.
- UI typography should be cleaner and more neutral than the literary content typography.
- Reader mode should favor long-form readability over decorative display.

## Layout & Spacing

- The center reading canvas is the dominant surface.
- Discovery rail and metadata/actions rail should support the reader, not compete with it.
- Mobile should collapse secondary rails into drawers or sheets while preserving the reading surface as the main stage.
- The bottom composer must feel like an intentional dock, not an unrelated floating widget.

## Elevation & Depth

- Use depth sparingly.
- The reading canvas may feel slightly lifted from the shell.
- Overlays such as search, filters, or editor expansion should be crisp and controlled rather than glassy or ornamental.

## Shapes

- Prefer restrained geometry: subtle rounding, clean separators, strong rhythm.
- Avoid overly soft blog-card styling.

## Components

### Reading canvas
- Large, quiet, typographically focused.
- Supports both continuous and paged experiences.

### Discovery rail
- Dense but readable.
- Prioritize title, snippet/first line, and type indicators.

### Bottom composer
- Compact by default.
- Expandable into a richer editor.

### Ratings
- Clear numeric expression.
- Separate visual treatment for creator rating and reader aggregate.

### Title art
- Treated as a hero surface when present.
- Must not make the shell visually noisy.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do
- Make the product feel like reading comes first.
- Let content styling shine more than shell styling.
- Keep the chrome neutral and elegant.

### Do not
- Turn the public experience into a dashboard.
- Overuse gradients or bright accents in the shell.
- Make basic reading dependent on opening nested views.
